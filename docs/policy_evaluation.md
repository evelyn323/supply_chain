# Policy Evaluation
## Inventory Metrics
- Fill Rate
- Stockout Days
- Average on-hand inventory
## Cost Metrics
- Holding Cost
- Stockout Cost
- Total Cost
## Policy Comparison
Compare each inventory policy under the same realized demand, lead time, initial conditions, and cost assumptions.
## Forecast Accuracy vs Inventory Performance
Evaluate whether the model with the lowest forecast error also produces the lower total inventory cost or highest service level.
## Sensitivity Analysis
- Lead time
- Safety stock
- Stockout penalty
- Holding cost
- Forecast bias or demand volatility (stretch)
