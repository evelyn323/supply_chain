# Policy Evaluation
## Inventory Metrics
- Unit Fill Rate: total demand immediately fulfilled divided by total demand.
- Stockout Days: number of days in which on-hand inventory is zero after demand is realized.
## Cost Metrics
- Holding Cost: daily holding cost computed from ending inventory.
- Stockout Penalty: penalty applied to unmet demand under the lost-sales assumption.
- Total Cost: holding cost plus stockout penalty.
## Policy Comparison
Compare each inventory policy under the same realized demand, lead time, initial conditions, and cost assumptions.
## Forecast Accuracy vs Inventory Performance
Evaluate whether the model with the lowest forecast error also produces the lower total inventory cost or highest service level.
## Sensitivity Analysis
- Fixed lead time: 3, 5, 7 days
- Safety stock: 20, 40, 60 units
- Stockout penalty: 1.00, 2.00, 5.00 per unmet unit
- Holding cost: 0.05, 0.10, 0.20 per unit per day
- Forecast bias or demand volatility can be examined as an extension
